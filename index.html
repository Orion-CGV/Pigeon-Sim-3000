HOW TO PLAY

Basic Controls

WASD Move around
Mouse Look around
Space Jump
E Interact
ESC Return to menu

Level 1 - Pigeon Simulator

Experience life as a city pigeon in this chaotic urban simulator! Fly through urban environments, poop on unsuspecting pedestrians, and steal food from street vendors and restaurants. Master the art of being a mischievous bird. Use F to toggle flying mode, WASD to move, and mouse to look around.

Level 2 - Speed Delivery Game

Become a delivery driver racing against the clock in a charming small town! Navigate tight streets, dodge traffic, and deliver packages as fast as possible. Master your route and use boost strategically to become the fastest delivery driver. Use W/S to accelerate/brake, A/D to steer, Space for boost, Q to cycle camera views, L for headlights (night only), and H for day/night toggle.

Level 3 - Gravity Cube Game

Control a mysterious cube with gravity-defying abilities in this mind-bending puzzle adventure! Rotate, flip, and manipulate the cube through increasingly complex puzzles. Master physics and spatial reasoning to unlock each level's secrets and complete the game. Use WASD to move and mouse to look around.
